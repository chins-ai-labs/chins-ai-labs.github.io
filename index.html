DEFECT CLASSIFICATION

We don't just find defects.
We log their type, coordinates, and images.

Major defect types are classified automatically, including wrinkles, holes/pinholes, foreign matter, scratches, dents/impressions, oxidation/discoloration, stains, and Cr drag-out.
